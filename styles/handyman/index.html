The jobs on the list

Small repairs and fix-ups around the house. Anything that needs a licensed trade, Gary will tell you and point you to one.

  • Shelves and hanging

    Shelves, mirrors, curtain rails and TVs, fixed level.

  • Doors and locks

    Sticking doors re-hung, latches and handles replaced.

  • Decks and fences

    Loose boards, wonky palings and broken gate latches.

  • Bathroom fix-ups

    Re-silicone, tap washers, towel rails and grout touch-ups.

  • Flat-pack assembly

    Wardrobes, beds, desks and bookcases, built and fixed to the wall.

  • Everything else

    The odd jobs that do not fit anywhere. Ask, he will say yes or no.

How a half day goes

Four steps. You do the first one.

  1. 01

    Send the list

    Text a photo or a few lines. As rough as you like.

  2. 02

    Pick a morning

    Gary tells you which morning fits and how long the list will take.

  3. 03

    Door opens, tools out

    He starts at the top and works down.

  4. 04

    Tick, tidy, done

    You walk round with him, and he sweeps up before he goes.

Who turns up

Gary works alone. You get him, not a different pair of hands each time, and he gives you a straight answer about how long the list will take before he starts.

He keeps his own ladders, drop sheets and a vacuum in the van. If a job needs a plumber, electrician or builder, he says so on the day and does not guess.

What Gary says on the phone

“Send me the list and a photo of the worst one. I will tell you straight away if it is a half day or a whole day.”

Where he works

Waiuku and the localities around it, plus a run out along the Pukekohe road. Further out by arrangement.

Glenbrook, Otaua, Matakawau, Pollok, Awhitu, Orua Bay, Karioitahi and the town centre, and more.

Send a photo from anywhere in the area and Gary will say whether it is on his route that week.
