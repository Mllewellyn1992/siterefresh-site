What we fix

Cars, utes and small vans, petrol and diesel. We tell you what we find before we touch anything, and you say yes first.

  • Servicing

    Oil, filters, fluids and a written check of the lot.

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors, fluid and lines, measured and shown to you.

  • Diagnostics

    Scan tool reads, then a hands-on check to find the cause.

  • Pre-WoF checks

    We check the usual fail points and fix what we find before the test.

  • Suspension

    Shocks, springs, bushes and joints, for clunks and uneven wear.

  • Tyres and wheels

    Fitting, balancing and a wheel alignment check.
