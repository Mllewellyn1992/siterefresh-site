What we take

Most things that fit on a truck and are allowed at the tip. Ask first about anything else.

  1. 01

    Furniture

    Couches, beds, tables and wardrobes, carried out from anywhere in the house.

  2. 02

    Whiteware

    Fridges, washers and dryers, taken off your hands and recycled where we can.

  3. 03

    Household rubbish

    Bags and boxes from a clear-out, a shed, a spare room or a flat.

  4. 04

    Green waste

    Branches, hedge clippings and lawn cuttings, loaded and tipped.

  5. 05

    Building waste

    Offcuts, old decking and gib from a small reno, in tidy loads.

  6. 06

    Section clear-outs

    An overgrown corner, a collapsed shed or a yard that needs starting again.
