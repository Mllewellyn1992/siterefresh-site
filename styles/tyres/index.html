What we fit

Everything that goes between the car and the road, at one yard in Hāwera.

  • New tyres

    Sizes for cars, SUVs, utes and small trucks.

  • Puncture repairs

    Plugged and patched from the inside, then rechecked.

  • Wheel alignment

    Camber, caster and toe set to the car's own figures.

  • Balancing

    Weights fitted to stop the shake at 100 km/h.

  • Mags and rims

    New and second-hand wheels, straightened and refitted.

  • Mobile call-out

    A flat on the roadside, fitted where you are.

How a fitting goes

Four steps. Most cars are on and off the hoist in under an hour.

  1. 01

    Ring or text

    Give Sam the size on your sidewall and the car.

  2. 02

    Check the tread

    He measures what is left and shows you the wear.

  3. 03

    Fit and balance

    Old tyres off, new tyres on, each wheel balanced.

  4. 04

    Torque and go

    Nuts torqued to spec, pressures set, and you drive off.

Sam runs Roadhold on his own with one apprentice, out of a single yard in Hāwera. You deal with the person who measures the tread and torques the nuts.
