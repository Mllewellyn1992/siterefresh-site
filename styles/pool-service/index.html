What we look after

Home pools and spas, chlorine and salt. You get the same two people each visit and a sheet of what they found.

  • Weekly pool care

    Test, skim, brush and vacuum, same day each week.

  • Spa and swim spa care

    Drain, clean, refill and balance on a schedule you pick.

  • Green pool rescue

    Shock, filter and rebalance a green pool back to clear.

  • Filter and pump

    Cartridge and sand filter cleans, plus a pump and basket check.

  • Chemical balancing

    Test and correct pH, chlorine and alkalinity, and show you the result.

  • Summer start-up

    Cover off, clean, fill, balance and run, ready to swim.

Two people, one pool.

Ngaire and Josh run Bluebottle from their home in Pukekohe. They keep a card for every pool they look after with its volume, its equipment and its last six readings, so each visit starts where the last one stopped.
