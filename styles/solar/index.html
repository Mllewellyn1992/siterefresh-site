what we install

Residential first, with a few small farm sheds and offices. Every system is designed from your roof and your bill, not a template.

  • roof solar

    Panels sized to your roof and your daytime use.

  • batteries

    Store the afternoon's power for the evening.

  • car chargers

    A wall charger run from your switchboard, tested.

  • inverter swaps

    Replace a tired inverter and keep your panels.

  • monitoring

    See what your roof made today, from your phone.

  • system checks

    A yearly look at the panels, wiring and output.

how a system goes in

Most homes are on the roof for one day. The design takes longer than the install, and that's on purpose.

  1. 01

    look

    Anna checks the roof, the shade and your last twelve bills. Free.

  2. 02

    design

    A written design with the panel layout, output and what it saves.

  3. 03

    install

    One crew, usually one day on the roof, a second inside.

  4. 04

    switch on

    Tested with you, app set up, and a call a month later.
