What Dan fixes

Commercial and home refrigeration, plus the heat pumps that run on the same gas and the same pipes.

  • Cool rooms

    Walk-in chillers and freezers for cafés, butchers and growers.

  • Fridges and freezers

    Upright, under-bench and chest units, repaired where they stand.

  • Heat pumps

    Servicing, fault finding and regassing for wall units and ducted systems.

  • Display cabinets

    Shop and café counters, from door seals to fan motors.

  • Ice machines

    Descaled, cleaned and sanitised, with water and drain checks.

  • Breakdown callouts

    One call, a plan before Dan arrives, and parts on the van.

How a callout goes

Four steps from the phone call to a stable temperature.

  1. 01

    Call

    Ring Dan, tell him the make and what it is doing.

  2. 02

    Triage

    He tells you what to move or keep shut, and gives an arrival time.

  3. 03

    Fix

    Fault found on site. Common parts come out of the van.

  4. 04

    Check

    Dan stays until the temperature is holding and writes it on your log.

Dan runs Coldfront on his own out of Gore. You get the engineer who answers the phone, not a call centre.
