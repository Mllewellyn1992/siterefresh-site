What we run

Cabling for homes and small businesses. Jordan measures, quotes in writing, and certifies every run he installs.

  • $ run --cat6 --outlets

    Cat6 outlets

    New wall outlets run back to one cupboard, one cable per port.

  • $ patch --panel --rack

    Patch panels and racks

    Tidy termination in a wall cabinet or rack, labelled to match the outlets.

  • $ mount --wifi --ap

    Wi-Fi access points

    Ceiling and wall points on their own cable, so Wi-Fi covers the whole building.

  • $ link --camera --poe

    Camera and door cabling

    Cable for security cameras and door stations, powered over the same cable.

  • $ certify --all

    Testing and fault-finding

    Tracing slow or dead ports, with a printed pass or fail for every run.

  • $ tidy --label --move

    Tidy-ups and moves

    Untangling a cupboard, labelling every cable and moving outlets.

How a job runs

Most homes and small offices are surveyed in one visit and cabled in one or two days. The test report is yours to keep.

~ $ survey --site 14-chambers-st
  6 outlets, 1 cupboard, 38 m longest run
~ $ run --cable cat6 --label on
  OUT-01 .. OUT-06   pulled, clipped, labelled
~ $ terminate --panel pp-24 --outlets all
  6 outlets punched down, T568B
~ $ certify --report report.pdf
  6 / 6 pass
