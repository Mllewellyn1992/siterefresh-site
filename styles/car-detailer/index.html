Services

Cars, SUVs and utes. Pick a package or tell Cam what the car needs, and he brings everything to you.

  • Exterior hand wash

    Two-bucket wash, wheels and arches, dried by hand.

  • Interior detail

    Vacuum, steam and wipe of seats, carpets, dash and doors.

  • Machine polish

    Clay, then a machine polish to lift swirls and restore gloss.

  • Sealant and wax

    A hand-applied sealant or wax to keep water and dirt off.

  • Headlight restore

    Sand and polish cloudy lenses back to clear, then seal.

  • Leather care

    Clean and condition leather seats, with a note of any wear.

A visit

Most cars take two to four hours. We work in the shade if there is some, and clean up our own water.
