What we paint

Signs for shops, farms, pubs and boats. Drawn full size, painted by hand, and built to stand the West Coast weather.

  • Shop signs

    Fascia boards and hanging signs, painted to your name and style.

  • Van and ute lettering

    Doors, tailgates and trailers lettered by hand, not vinyl.

  • Gold-leaf windows

    Gilded letters on the glass that catch the light in the afternoon.

  • Wall signs

    Big painted lettering on brick, block and corrugated iron.

  • Boat names

    Names and port of registry, drawn and painted at the wharf.

  • Sign restoration

    Faded old signs repainted to match the original lettering.

How a sign gets made

Most signs take one to two weeks from visit to finished job. Nothing is painted until you have seen the layout at full size.

  1. 1

    Visit

    Sam measures the board or wall, photographs it and talks lettering.

  2. 2

    Draw

    A layout at full size on paper. You see it before paint touches anything.

  3. 3

    Paint

    Enamel and brush, one colour at a time, left to dry between coats.

  4. 4

    Fit

    Hung or painted on site, then sealed against sun and salt.
