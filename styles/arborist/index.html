What we do

Residential and lifestyle blocks, from one overgrown hedge to a row of shelterbelt pines. Climbing gear, a bucket truck and a chipper on every job.

  • Tree felling

    Whole trees down in sections near houses and lines, or straight-felled where there's room. Pines, gums, macrocarpa, the lot.

    Climbed and rigged, or bucket truck to 17 m

  • Pruning

    Crown lifts, thinning for light and views, deadwood out.

  • Stump grinding

    Ground below the lawn and back-filled.

  • Box hedge squared up by hand

  • Hedge trimming

    Griselinia, privet, pittosporum, box. Tall hedges off a platform.

  • Storm clean-up

    Trees on fences, roofs and driveways. We answer the phone.

  • Firewood

    Split, seasoned pine and gum by the trailer load, delivered around town.
