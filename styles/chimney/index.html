Burning well

Below 120°C, smoke turns to tar in the flue.

A slow, smouldering fire keeps the flue cool, and creosote builds where it's coolest. A hot first half-hour keeps it clean between sweeps.

< 120°C

Creosote zone. Tar forms; the flue blackens and narrows.

150–250°C

A clean burn. Aim to sit here once the fire is going.

250–400°C

Hot start. Fine for the first 20 minutes, then turn it down.

> 400°C

Overfiring. Close the air off; it warps baffles and cracks glass.
