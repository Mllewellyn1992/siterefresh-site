What Mel cleans

Homes first, a few small offices after hours. Every visit runs off the same checklist, so nothing gets missed on a busy week.

  • Regular house cleans

    Weekly or fortnightly, same day, same cleaner. Kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting and beds.

    Weekly · fortnightly

  • End-of-tenancy cleans

    The full list your property manager checks, inside cupboards, ovens, skirtings and windows.

    Bond-ready checklist

  • Spring cleans

    A deep top-to-bottom once a year: light fittings, walls, behind the fridge, curtains taken down.

    1 to 2 days

  • Oven cleaning

    Racks soaked, glass taken apart where it comes apart, baked-on grease off without harsh fumes.

    About 2 hours

  • Window cleaning

    Inside and out on single-storey homes, tracks and sills vacuumed, screens rinsed.

    Ground floor + ladder

  • Office cleans

    Small offices and clinics after 5:30 pm. Desks, kitchenette, toilets, bins and floors.

    After 17:30
