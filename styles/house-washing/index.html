What we wash

Houses first, then everything that stands around them. Every job is looked at, priced in writing and washed to suit the surface.

  • House wash

    Walls, soffits and joinery washed soft and rinsed clean.

  • Roof treatment

    Moss and lichen killed, then rinsed off gently.

  • Decks

  • Fences

  • Gutters

    Cleared out, flushed, and checked for drips.

How a house wash goes

Four passes over the house. The treatment does the work, the water just carries it away.

  1. 01

    Look

    Cam checks the cladding, the roof and the plants, free of charge.

  2. 02

    Treat

    A gentle wash is sprayed on, and left to kill the moss and mould.

  3. 03

    Rinse

    Low pressure rinse from top to bottom, plants covered first.

  4. 04

    Check

    A walk-round with you, and a tidy of the path before we leave.

Which wash, where

Pressure is for concrete. Everything else gets a soft wash, because the wrong wash is how paint, roofs and timber get damaged.

SurfaceWash we useNever
WeatherboardSoft wash and low rinseHigh pressure
RoofMoss treatment, gentle rinseBlasting the surface
DeckSoft wash along the grainGouging the timber
ConcreteSurface cleaner, higher pressureChemicals near plants
