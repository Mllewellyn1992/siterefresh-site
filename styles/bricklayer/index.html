The bond drawer

The bond is the pattern the bricks lock in. It changes how a wall looks and how it carries load. Hemi lays all three.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Hemi walks the site, checks the plans and levels, and writes down the bond, brick and mortar.

  2. 02

    Set out

    Corners built first, a line strung between them, every course checked against it.

  3. 03

    Lay

    Brick and block laid in the agreed bond, ties and flashings fitted as the wall goes up.

  4. 04

    Point and clean

    Joints struck to match the sample, then the wall washed down and the site swept.
