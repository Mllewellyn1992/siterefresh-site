Fifteen years on, still fixing the quake.

A report from the repair side of the business.

A good share of the work that comes through the door is still from the earthquakes. Some houses were never repaired. More were repaired fast, in a hurry, by whoever was available, and the floor has started to slope again.

Mark starts every repair with a floor-level survey and a look under the house. If the piles have dropped, they are re-packed or replaced; if the ground is poor, an engineer designs the foundation before a price is written. You get the survey, the engineer's notes and the quote together, in plain words.

Repairs on a house with an insurance claim open are fine. We work to the scope your insurer approves and tell you, in writing, if we find anything outside it.
