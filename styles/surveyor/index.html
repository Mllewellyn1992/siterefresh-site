What we survey

Work for homeowners, builders, farmers and developers. Each job ends with a plan you can hand to a council, a lawyer or a builder.

  • Boundary pegs

    Old pegs found, lost ones replaced, every corner marked.

  • Title plans

    Plans for subdivisions, boundary changes and new titles.

  • Contour surveys

    Levels and features of a site, drawn as a contour plan.

  • Building set-out

    Corners and levels pegged so the build starts square.

  • Flats and easements

    Unit plans, cross-lease updates and right-of-way easements.

  • Pre-purchase checks

    Does the fence match the title? We measure it before you sign.

How a boundary survey goes

Most residential jobs are measured in a single day and the plan arrives by email. The numbers below are from a made-up section, to show what you receive.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Tell us the address and what you need. We check the title and old plans first.

  2. 02

    Measure

    On site with the instrument: we find the old marks and measure from them.

  3. 03

    Calculate

    Back at the desk the figures are closed and checked against the title.

  4. 04

    Plan

    You get the plan and a short report. New pegs are in the ground by then.
