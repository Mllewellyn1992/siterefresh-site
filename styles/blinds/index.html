Blinds and curtains

Measured, made to size and fitted by Rachel, for homes and small offices.

  • Roller blinds

    Plain and blockout fabrics, cut to fit the reveal.

  • Venetian blinds

    Timber and aluminium slats you can tilt to taste.

  • Curtains

    Made-to-measure drapes, lined or sheer, hung on tracks or rods.

  • Day-and-night

    Sheer and solid bands in one blind, for light and privacy.

  • Motorised blinds

    Remote or phone control for the hard-to-reach windows.

  • Repairs and restring

    Cords, brackets and chains on blinds already in your home.

How it works

Four steps, measured before anything is cut.

  1. 1

    Measure at home

    Rachel measures every window and checks the reveal, the wall and the light.

  2. 2

    Swatches in your light

    Fabric samples stay with you, so you see them morning and evening.

  3. 3

    Made to size

    Blinds and curtains are cut and sewn to your measurements, not off a shelf.

  4. 4

    Fitted and tested

    She fits, levels and runs every blind up and down before she leaves.

Measure twice. Hang once. Level to the millimetre.

Rachel measures every window twice, on different days if she can, and fits only when both numbers agree.
