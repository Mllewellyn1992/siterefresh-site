How a bathroom goes in

Most full bathrooms take two to three weeks from strip-out to the last bead of silicone.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Jo visits, measures and talks through the layout. Free, about an hour.

  2. 02

    Strip

    Everything out to the framing, so we see what's really behind the walls.

  3. 03

    Seal and tile

    Membrane first, then tiles, so the water has nowhere to go but the drain.

  4. 04

    Fit out

    Vanity, shower, taps and glass, then a clean and a walk-through with you.
