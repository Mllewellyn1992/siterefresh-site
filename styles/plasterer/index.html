What we plaster

Inside work first, with some exterior rendering. Every job is looked at in person and priced in writing.

  • Skim coating

    A smooth finish over old walls, ready for paint.

  • Ceilings

    New board, patched or recoated, with no joins showing.

  • Board stopping

    Tape, bed and finish on new plasterboard, joint by joint.

  • Patch repairs

    Holes, cracks and door knocks made good so they vanish.

  • Cornice

    Cove and ornate cornice, cut, fixed and set to match.

  • Exterior render

    Textured plaster over block and cladding, coloured to suit.

How a plaster job goes

A lounge usually takes three working days from first coat to paint. The wall needs time between coats, not just hands.

  1. 01

    Look

    Hine checks the wall, the board and the damp. Free, about 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Prep and stop

    Fill, tape and bed the joints, sand back, mask the floor.

  3. 03

    Skim

    Two thin coats trowelled flat, the second once the first has pulled in.

    Drying starts here
  4. 04

    Dry and paint

    Even pale colour means it's dry. Then it's yours to paint.

Is it dry yet?

The wall tells you. A skim coat goes from dark and patchy to an even pale, and paint only goes on at the last stage.

Colours are a guide for this sample, drying time depends on the weather.

  • Wet

    Dark, with damp patches. Don't touch.

  • Drying

    Pale with a few dark spots. Wait a bit longer.

  • Dry

    Even and pale. Sand lightly, then prime.
