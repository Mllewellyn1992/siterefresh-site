what we fit and fix

New doors, repairs and openers for homes and small workshops. Tama measures, prices in writing and does the work himself.

  • new sectional doors

    Insulated panel doors in your choice of colour, fitted with new tracks.

  • roller doors

    Steel roller doors for garages, sheds and workshops, built to your opening.

  • openers and remotes

    New motors, wall buttons and remotes, with a battery backup if you want one.

  • spring and cable repairs

    Broken torsion springs and frayed cables, swapped safely and tested.

  • tracks and panels

    Bent tracks straightened, dented panels replaced, worn rollers swapped.

  • safety checks

    Auto-reverse, balance and spring check on an existing door, with a written note.

which door suits

Three common types, what each is good at, and what you will hear when it opens.

  • sectional

    insulatedquietspace in front

    Rises on tracks and rests along the ceiling. Quiet and well sealed, the usual pick for an attached garage.

  • roller

    compacttight spacestough

    Winds up into a drum above the opening. Good where there is no room for a door to swing or lift.

  • tilt door

    simplereplace in kind

    A one-piece door that swings up and out. We keep older tilt doors running and replace them when parts run out.

how a door goes in

A new door is usually measured in one visit and fitted in one day. You can use it the same afternoon.

  1. 01

    measure

    Tama checks the opening, headroom and sideroom, free and without obligation.

  2. 02

    order

    Door made to size in your colour. Usually here within two weeks.

  3. 03

    fit

    Old door out, new tracks and springs in, door hung and balanced.

  4. 04

    test

    Auto-reverse and balance checked, remotes set up, then a walk-through.
