The hire list.

Everything we drop is delivered, set up, checked and collected by us. Book early for school holidays and summer weekends.

  • Bouncy castles

    From a toddler castle to a big jungle one, all with netted sides.

  • Slides

    Dry slides and combo castles with a slide on the end.

  • Marquees

    Pole and frame marquees for the lawn, sides and flooring extra.

  • Tables and chairs

    Trestle tables and plastic chairs, by the table or the dozen.

  • Candyfloss and popcorn

    Machines with sugar and kernels for the first 40 serves.

  • Obstacle courses

    Tunnels, walls and a finish line, for bigger kids and school days.

Will it fit?

Tell us the space. We bring the tape. A castle needs flat ground, a clear metre round it and a power point within 20 metres.

  • Toddler castle3 × 3 mUnder-5s, with a low wall
  • Jungle castle4 × 4 mMost backyards, ages 3 to 12
  • Castle and slide6 × 4 mNeeds a straight run, no steep slope
  • Marquee3 × 6 mPegged into grass, or weighted on concrete

About us

Kiri and Tama run Jumbo Jumps from their garage in Whakatāne. Every castle is cleaned, dried and checked between hires, and one of them is always on the end of the phone on party day.
