How a mow goes

Ben texts you before he comes and again when he's done. If rain moves the day, he'll tell you.

Never more than a third

Taking off too much in one go stresses the grass and shows up brown. Ben mows often enough that he only ever takes the top third, and leaves the lawn at a healthy height for the season.
