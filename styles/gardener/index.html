What we do

Weekly, fortnightly or one-off. We bring the tools and take the green waste away.

  • Regular garden care

    A set visit every week or fortnight to keep it all ticking.

  • Lawns and edges

    Mowed, edged and trimmed to a clean line along beds and paths.

  • Hedge trimming

    Square, tapered or loose, cut by hand and tidied up after.

  • Pruning

    Roses, fruit trees and shrubs cut back at the right time of year.

  • Weeding and mulch

    Beds cleared of weeds, then covered to hold moisture and keep them away.

  • Planting and tidy-ups

    New plants in, overgrown corners cleared, section made presentable.
