How much rain your roof takes

Invercargill gets about 1,100 mm of rain a year. Every litre of it has to run off your roof, along your spouting and down a pipe. Set your roof size and see what that means, month by month.

A standard house is stripped and re-roofed in 2 to 4 days. You stay in the house; the roof is closed in every night.
