How a job runs

Most platforms and driveways are cut in two to four days, weather allowing.

  1. 01

    Walk the site

    Hamish walks the ground with you, checks access, levels and where the water goes. Free, about 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Written price

    Hours, machine and trucking set out, with what is and isn't included.

  3. 03

    Peg and cut

    Levels pegged first, then the digger starts at the top and works down.

  4. 04

    Shape and tidy

    Batters trimmed, drains in, spoil gone, and a walk round with you before we leave.

About Hamish

One operator, one digger, and the same hands on every job.

Hamish runs Longacre Earthmoving himself out of Maungatapere. He walks every site before quoting, runs the digger himself, and rings you if the ground isn't what he expected.

He works rural blocks and town sections alike, and keeps the machine clean so it doesn't carry weeds from one farm to the next.
