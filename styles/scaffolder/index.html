What goes up

Residential and light commercial scaffold. Every job is measured, priced in writing, erected by the same crew and tagged before anyone climbs it.

  • House scaffold

    Full wrap for painting, cladding and re-roofing, in steel with boarded lifts.

  • Roof edge protection

    Rails and toe boards round the eaves, up before the first tile comes off.

  • Painter's platforms

    Low lifts and boards for fascias, soffits and second-storey walls.

  • Chimney towers

    Narrow towers for chimneys, gable ends and tight side yards.

  • Mobile tower hire

    Wheeled towers for owner-builders, with a handover on how to use them.

  • Weather wrap

    Shrink-wrap over scaffold to keep the weather off roof and wall work.

About Tussock

Hemi started the business with one truck and a set of boards. It is still his crew on every job, and they erect and strip what they quote. If the ground is soft or the access is tight, you hear it on the measure, not on the day.
