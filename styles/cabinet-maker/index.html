How a cabinet is made

Every job starts as a drawing you can mark up. Nothing is cut until the dimensions are agreed.

What goes into a drawing

  1. Site measure: walls, floors and ceilings checked for square
  2. Layout drawn so you can see doors, drawers and gaps
  3. Every part dimensioned, nothing left to guess on site
  4. You mark it up, Nik redraws it, you sign it off
  5. Cutting list made from the signed drawing, and nothing else
  1. 01

    Measure

    Nik visits, measures and talks through how you use the space.

  3. 03

    Build

    Made in his workshop from the signed-off drawing.

  4. 04

    Fit

    Installed, doors hung and adjusted, and the site left clean.
