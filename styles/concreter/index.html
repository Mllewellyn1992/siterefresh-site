What we pour

Residential work from a single path to a full house slab. Every job is measured by Dave, priced in writing, and boxed, poured and finished by the same crew.

  • Driveways

    Plain grey or coloured, with sawn joints and a broom or float finish.

    125 mm on 150 mm basecourse

  • Paths

    Front paths, side paths and clothesline pads, laid to fall away from the house.

    75–100 mm, 1:100 fall

  • Exposed aggregate

    Washed to show the stone, sealed after cure. Pick from 6 local stone mixes.

    Washed at 4–8 hours, sealed at 28 days

  • House slabs

    Raft and rib slabs for new builds and extensions, poured to the engineer's plan.

    Pre-pour check booked with council

  • Retaining walls

    Poured and block-fill walls with drainage behind, for banks and level lawns.

    Up to 1.5 m without consent

  • Concrete cutting

    Control joints, trenching for drains and cutting out cracked panels to re-pour.

    Cuts to 150 mm deep

How a driveway goes in

Most driveways are dug, boxed and poured inside a week. You can walk on it after 2 days and drive on it after 7.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Dave walks the site, checks levels and drainage. Free, about 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Box

    Dig out, compact basecourse, set boxing and mesh. Usually 1–2 days.

  3. 03

    Pour

    Truck booked for the morning, finished by the afternoon.

  4. 04

    Cut

    Joints sawn within 24 hours so cracks land where we want them.
