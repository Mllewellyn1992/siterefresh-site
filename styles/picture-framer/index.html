What we frame

Pictures, prints, objects and mirrors. Fiona measures, quotes in writing and does the framing herself.

  • Picture framing

    Photographs, prints and paintings, framed to suit the picture and the wall.

  • Mounts and mats

    Cut-bevel mounts in single, double or triple layers, in acid-free board.

  • Canvas stretching

    Canvases and needlework stretched square and taut over a new frame.

  • Shadow boxes

    Deep frames for medals, jerseys, tickets and keepsakes, pinned in place.

  • Mirror frames

    Mirrors cut and framed to size, with a hanging kit that fits the wall.

  • Re-framing and repairs

    New glass, new backing or a whole new frame for something already on your wall.
