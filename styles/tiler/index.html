What Priya tiles

Homes, not shopfronts. One room at a time, measured and set out on site before a price is written.

  • 01

    Bathroom tiling

    Floors, walls and showers, from a single wall to a full strip-out and retile.

    Walls to ceiling
  • 02

    Kitchen splashbacks

    Subway, mosaic sheet or large format, cut neatly round sockets and the window reveal.

    Usually 1 day
  • 03

    Floor tiling

    Kitchens, laundries and hallways, levelled first so the tiles sit flat and don't crack.

    Up to 1200 × 600
  • 04

    Waterproofing

    A brushed-on membrane under every shower and wet floor, left to cure before tiling.

    2 coats, 24 h cure
  • 05

    Outdoor tiles

    Courtyards, porches and pool surrounds in frost-rated porcelain with a non-slip finish.

    R11 slip rating
  • 06

    Re-grouting

    Old, cracked or mouldy grout raked out and replaced, then sealed. Often saves a full retile.

    Half a day for most showers
