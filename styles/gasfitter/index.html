What Sam does

Gas work for homes, from one hob to a full gas hot water swap. Each job is measured, priced in writing and tested at the end.

  • Hobs and cookers

    New gas hobs and freestanding cookers fitted and tested.

  • Gas heaters

    Wall and flued heaters installed, serviced and cleaned.

  • Gas hot water

    Continuous flow and storage units swapped in a day.

  • LPG bottles

    Bottle hook-ups, regulators and hoses set up safely.

  • Safety checks

    A line-by-line check of your gas appliances, in writing.

  • Leak finding

    Tracing the smell or the drop in pressure, then fixing it.

About Sam

One gasfitter, one van, and a note left on the bench.

Sam works on his own out of Taupō, mostly on home hobs, heaters and hot water. He answers his own phone, comes to look before he quotes, and tells you if a job isn't worth doing.

If something is unsafe he'll make it safe first and talk about the rest after.
