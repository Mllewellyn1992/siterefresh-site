What we mend

Everyday repairs and full services for most bikes. If it needs a part, Hemi tells you before ordering it.

  • Puncture repair

    New tube or patch, tyre checked for glass and thorns.

  • Brakes

    Pads, cables and adjustment so it stops straight and fast.

  • Gears and chain

    Indexing, new chain and cassette, and a clean drivetrain.

  • Wheel truing

    Buckled or wobbling wheels spun true on the stand.

  • Full service

    Every bolt, bearing and cable checked, then a test ride.

  • E-bike checks

    Brakes, tyres and drivetrain on bikes with a motor.

How a repair goes

Most repairs are done in two to three days. Hemi rings or texts when yours is ready.

Six points on every bike

  1. Tyre wear and pressure
  2. Front and rear brakes
  3. Chain wear and gear indexing
  4. Headset and stem tight
  5. Wheels true, spokes even
  6. Cables and housing frayed or sticky

Hemi runs through these before he hands a bike back, whatever it came in for.

    Drop it in
