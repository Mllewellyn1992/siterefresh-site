What Tom lays

Floors for homes, from one bedroom of carpet to a whole house of timber. Every job is measured, priced in writing and cleaned up after.

  • Timber and engineered

    Solid and engineered boards, nailed or glued down.

  • Vinyl plank and laminate

    Click-lock planks floated over a prepped floor.

  • Carpet

    Stretched in with new underlay, seams hidden.

  • Sanding and finishing

    Old boards sanded back and sealed with a hard coat.

  • Levelling and underlay

    Dips filled and the base made ready before laying.

  • Stairs and landings

    Treads and nosings cut to fit, in timber or carpet.

How a floor goes down

  1. 01

    Measure

    Tom checks the room, the subfloor and the doors. Free, about 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Prep

    Old floor up, base levelled. Boards rest in the room to settle to its humidity.

  3. 03

    Lay

    Boards start from the straightest wall, gaps kept at the edges.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Trims and thresholds fitted, offcuts gone, and a walk-round with you.
